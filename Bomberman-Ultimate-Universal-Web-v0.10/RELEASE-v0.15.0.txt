Bomberman v0.15.0 — AI Overhaul

Gameplay/AI
- Persistent goals with hysteresis: attack, collect, clear, reposition, and intentional wait.
- Time-aware A* routing that can safely cross future blast lanes instead of treating all projected fire as immediately blocked.
- Live escape-route revalidation against the combined bomb/chain-reaction timeline.
- Short-horizon AI tile reservations with stable yielding to reduce collisions and head-on deadlocks.
- Edge-specific loop penalties and decision-state loop detection instead of broad row/column bans.
- Intentional waiting is recognized as a valid decision rather than a stuck state.
- Contestant-neutral target selection with short target commitment, reducing robotic human tunnel-vision.
- Bomb scoring rewards cutting off opponent escape options.
- Simulated opponents receive light personality biases while retaining fallible behavior.

Stability
- Replaces the legacy PreloadJS image queue at startup with native browser Image loading to prevent the permanent Loading screen seen on modern Chromium/local servers.
- Preserves the v0.14.4 simulated-opponent opening-pocket breakout fix.
- Uses a new v0.15.0 service-worker cache while keeping the same GitHub Pages path.
