Controls & help

Player 1: Arrow keys to move. Space places a normal bomb. WASD fires Bomb Kick when you have a charge.

Local Player 2: Follow the in-game control panel. Use the touch joystick and action buttons on mobile.

Destroy wood to discover power-ups. Keep an escape route open. Hearts absorb a hit, and Bomb Kick lets you send bombs through lanes.

Public matchmaking can use simulated players. Settings include AI difficulty and the AI testing room. Graphics lets you adjust rendering FPS and resolution.
