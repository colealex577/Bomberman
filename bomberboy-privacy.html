Bomberboy Ultimate Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Chrome extension version 2.2.2

Bomberboy Ultimate is a standalone arcade game published by Cole Alexander. This policy describes the current Chrome extension release, including its local game features and optional online multiplayer.

Data stored locally

The game saves your chosen display name, game-generated player identifier, settings, character appearance, rating, wins and losses, other gameplay progress, and information about players you encounter in your browser's local storage. It uses this information to maintain your game experience, rankings, preferences and multiplayer player directory. It does not access browsing history, the content of other websites, email, personal files, health data, or payment information. Local game data remains in the browser until you clear it or remove the extension and its stored data.

Online multiplayer and chat

When you choose online multiplayer or online player discovery, the game uses PeerJS signaling and WebRTC connections. Your chosen display name, game-generated identifier, connection metadata, game ratings, wins and losses, last-online timestamps, gameplay state, gameplay inputs, character appearance and in-game chat may be exchanged as necessary for matchmaking, friends/player discovery, and matches. The game's player directory can exchange known player profiles and recent activity with other connected players; those players may retain this information in their local game storage. In-game messages are visible to the players you communicate with. Avoid using a real name or sharing sensitive personal information in your game name or chat.

Network connection information, including IP addresses, may be processed by the PeerJS signaling service, Google STUN services and Metered Open Relay TURN infrastructure used to establish or relay WebRTC connections. Other connected players may also learn your network address through WebRTC. These services have their own privacy practices. See PeerJS, Google's privacy policy, and Metered. The publisher does not operate a central chat archive. You can use local single-player and simulated gameplay instead of real online multiplayer.

Statistics and analytics

Version 2.2.2 has no configured publisher statistics server and no Google Analytics integration. The optional reporting and Admin features cannot submit player statistics to the publisher in this release. If a future release enables reporting, the privacy policy and Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before collection begins, and users will be informed and asked to consent.

Use and sharing

Game information is used to provide and maintain the game's functionality. The publisher does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it for creditworthiness or lending. Data is shared with connected players and the networking services described above only as needed for game functionality, or when required by law or necessary for security. Bomberboy Ultimate's use of user data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Choices and contact

You can play offline, choose a pseudonymous username, clear local game data through Chrome, or uninstall the extension. Removing your local data does not automatically delete information already shared with other players or held by networking providers. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the Chrome Web Store support contact or the project support page. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public issue reports.
